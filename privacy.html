Privacy Policy

ChemoCian · Last updated 14 June 2026

ChemoCian ("the app") is a study aid for the UK Medical Oncology SCE, developed by Cian Murphy. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle. In short: the app does not collect personal data and has no user accounts.

Data we collect

None. The app has no sign-in, no account, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you.

Data stored on your device

Your quiz progress — questions answered, scores, flagged questions, and session history — is stored only on your device (using Apple's on-device storage). It is never uploaded to us or anyone else. You can erase it at any time with "Reset progress" in Settings, or by deleting the app.

Network use

The app downloads a static question bank over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection from Cloudflare R2 storage so that questions can be updated without an app update. This is a one-way download of a public file; the app does not send any personal data with the request. As with any internet request, Cloudflare may process standard technical information such as your IP address to deliver the file. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Contacting us

The app offers an optional "Submit feedback" link that opens your email app. If you choose to email us, we will see your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to respond to you.

Children

The app is intended for medical professionals and trainees and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
