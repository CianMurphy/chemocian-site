Privacy Policy

OncologySCE · Last updated 30 September 2026

OncologySCE ("the app") is a study aid for the UK Medical Oncology SCE. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and why. In short: the app has no user accounts, no advertising and no third party analytics or tracking SDKs. Your progress syncs to your own iCloud. A small amount of data reaches the developer: community answer statistics that do not name you, problem reports and emails you choose to send, the purchase check that unlocks the question bank, and, only if you opt in, research data held under a random code that we cannot connect to you.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Cian Murphy, the developer of OncologySCE, based in the United Kingdom. Contact: oncologysce@proton.me.

Your progress and history (your iCloud)

Your quiz progress (questions answered, scores, flagged questions, review schedule and session history) is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, it also syncs between your own devices through Apple's iCloud (iCloud key-value storage and your private CloudKit database). This copy lives in your iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot see or access it. You can erase it with "Reset progress" in Settings, by deleting the app, or through your iCloud storage settings.

Community answer statistics

After you answer a question, the app can show what percentage of other users chose each option. To make this work, the first time you answer each question the app saves a small record to the app's CloudKit public database (run by Apple). Each record holds the question's ID, the option you chose, whether it was correct, and a random ID created by the app on your device (not your name, email or Apple ID). CloudKit also notes which iCloud account created the record, using an opaque identifier that Apple does not reveal as your name or Apple ID.

The app only ever displays these records as totals and percentages. Because the database is public to the app, other copies of the app can read the individual records in order to count them, but those records contain nothing that names you. The records are kept while the app runs this feature. They are not used to build a profile of you. If you want your records removed, email us.

If you choose to share research answers (below), the app stops saving these records while answer sharing is on, and before it sends any answer for research it deletes the records your iCloud account has already saved for that question. This stops the two sets of data from being matched to each other. While you share, your answers no longer count towards the percentages, but you can still see them.

Reporting a problem

If you tap "Report a problem" on a question, the app sends the note you type, the question's ID and topic, and the app version to the developer using Apple's CloudKit public database. CloudKit associates the report with your iCloud account so duplicates can be followed up, but we do not see your name, email or Apple ID from it. We use reports only to find and fix errors in the question bank. Please do not include personal or patient information in the note. Reporting is optional and the app works fully without it.

Unlocking the question bank (purchase check)

The full question bank is downloaded from our server, a Cloudflare Worker, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Before it will send the bank, the app sends the Worker Apple's signed proof of purchase: the AppTransaction (showing this Apple Account got the app) and, for subscribers, the signed subscription transaction. The Worker checks Apple's signature and returns a short-lived access token, which the app stores in your device's Keychain. These proofs contain Apple's transaction identifiers, product and dates, but no name, email or Apple ID.

The Worker may log a transaction identifier briefly when a check fails, and it keeps a list of refunded or revoked transaction identifiers so that a refunded purchase cannot keep unlocking the bank. Apple also sends the Worker notices about subscription events (for example renewals and refunds), which record the product, dates, price and transaction identifiers. We use this only to run the subscription and prevent misuse. Payment itself is handled entirely by Apple.

Research data (optional)

You can choose to share your answers to help find out how scores in the app relate to real SCE results, and to improve the questions. This is off unless you turn it on. The app asks you when you first use it. If you close the question without answering, it asks again on a later launch, no more than three times in all on each device. Sharing your exam result is a separate choice, off unless you turn it on. Saying no changes nothing about how the app works for you.

What is shared, if you opt in

  • For each answer: the question's ID, the option you picked (or that you left it blank in a mock), the answer that was keyed correct at the time, how long you took, and whether you marked it as unsure.
  • For each session: the week it took place (the Monday of that week, not the day or time), its order among your sessions, and which topic or mode it covered.
  • Your past quiz history already on your device (or synced to it from your other devices), which is sent once when you opt in, then new sessions as you complete them.
  • Only if you separately agree: your SCE result for a sitting (for example September 2026), given as pass, fail, did not sit or prefer not to say, and optionally a score band (for example 400 to 424). The app never asks for your exact score.
  • Whether each of the two sharing choices is on or off.

What is not shared or stored

Your name, email address, Apple ID, phone number, device identifiers, device model, app version, location, or the exact date and time of any session. Our code does not read or store your IP address. Cloudflare, which runs the service, necessarily sees your IP address in transit to deliver the request, as it does for any internet request (see Network use below).

A random code, not you

When you opt in, the app makes a random code and a secret key. Your answers are stored under that code. The code and key stay on your devices and in your own iCloud key-value storage, so your devices share one code. The code is never shown to you, we never see it next to anything that identifies you, and the app never puts it in an email, a file or anything you can share. The secret key only stops anyone else from sending data under your code.

Research data is kept by a separate small service, apart from the one that checks purchases and delivers the question bank, with request logging turned off. We never combine research data with emails, problem reports, purchase checks, community answer statistics or anything else we hold. We will not try to find out who anyone is.

Because of all this we have no way to link research data to you, so we do not consider it personal data. That rests partly on the design above and partly on rules we keep ourselves: we never combine research data with anything else we hold, we only look at it as group summaries, and we never try to identify anyone. We treat it with care anyway.

What this means for you: we cannot find your answers later

Because we cannot tell which data is yours, we cannot find, show, correct or delete your shared answers once they have been sent, even if you ask us. This follows from not being able to identify you (the principle in UK GDPR Article 11). Please decide with that in mind. Your own progress in the app is unaffected and stays under your control as described above.

Stopping

You can stop sharing at any time in Settings, Research sharing. Turn off a choice and nothing more of that kind is sent; turn off both and nothing more is sent at all. Once both are off, data already sent stays under the random code, is left out of any later analysis, and is deleted when the retention period ends. Stopping has no effect on your use of the app.

Why, and on what basis

Purposes: to evaluate how performance in the app relates to SCE results, and to improve question quality (for example spotting questions that are unclear or wrongly keyed). We only collect it because you choose to share it, and you can stop at any time.

Who can see it, and what is published

Only the developer. It is not sold, not shared with other organisations, not used for advertising, and not used to make any decision about anyone. Any findings that are published or presented are group summaries only, and every group shown has at least five people in it.

Where it is stored

Research data is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database set to Cloudflare's EU jurisdiction, so it is stored in the European Union. Cloudflare provides the service under its Data Processing Addendum. Cloudflare is a US company and its global network carries the requests, so some handling may take place outside the UK and EU, covered by the safeguards in that addendum.

How long it is kept

Research data is deleted automatically 2 years after the last upload under its code.

Questions and complaints

Email oncologysce@proton.me with any question about research sharing. We will explain how it works, but we cannot look up any individual's data. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to deal with your concern first.

Network use

Requests to our Worker and question images are delivered by Cloudflare. As with any internet request, Cloudflare processes standard technical information such as your IP address to deliver it and to protect the service, and may keep short-lived technical logs. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Our own code does not store your IP address.

The optional ChatGPT panel opens OpenAI's website inside the app. Anything you do there, including signing in, is between you and OpenAI under OpenAI's privacy policy. The app does not send your progress or research data to OpenAI.

Contacting us

The app offers an optional "Submit feedback" link that opens your email app. If you choose to email us, we will see your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to respond to you.

Children

The app is intended for medical professionals and trainees and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a change affects research sharing, the app will ask you again before anything more is sent.
