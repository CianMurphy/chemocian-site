What is ChemoCian?

A multiple-choice question practice app for the UK Medical Oncology SCE. It offers tutor-style and timed mock-exam practice, per-topic coverage and performance tracking, and review of incorrect and flagged questions.

How are questions sourced?

Questions are written offline from a curated evidence base (ESMO guidelines, NICE Technology Appraisals, and related references) with AI assistance, then reviewed before being added to the bank. They are a study aid and may contain errors — always confirm against primary sources.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The app ships with a built-in question bank and works offline. When online, it quietly refreshes the bank so you get the latest questions.

How is my progress stored?

Entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings → "Reset progress". This cannot be undone.

I found a wrong answer or a typo

Please email the question text and what you think is correct — corrections are genuinely appreciated and improve the bank for everyone.

ChemoCian is an educational study aid only. It does not provide medical advice and must not be used for clinical decision-making or patient care.
